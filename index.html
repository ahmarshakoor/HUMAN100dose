Safety and sources

  • This is a decision-support draft. It does not replace clinical judgement.
  • Doses with a named source come from regulator labels (SmPC, FDA, TGA/Medsafe) or hospital monographs, not yet checked against BNFC. Doses marked draft, unverified have no source checked yet. Verify every dose before use.
  • Neonates (under 1 month) are blocked. Use a neonatal formulary.
  • Renal and hepatic adjustment is not built in yet. Adjust manually.
  • Brands and strengths must be checked against the DRAP registry.
  • Anaesthesia drugs (relaxants, reversal, atropine) give a single bolus for the standard indication only. Dose is not a substitute for titration to effect and monitoring.
